# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Thornbury Mining Corp. ("Thornbury") and Kingsmere Geoscience Inc. ("Kingsmere"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on the date on which it has been signed by both parties, being the later of the dates of signature below (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Kingsmere (the "Disclosing Party") intends to disclose Confidential Information to Thornbury (the "Receiving Party") for a possible review of Kingsmere's survey data for Thornbury's exploration programme (the "Purpose"). Thornbury will not disclose confidential information under this Agreement, which imposes obligations of confidentiality on Thornbury only.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Compelled Disclosure

3.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 4. Remedies

4.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

5.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.

5.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Province of Ontario.

5.3 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on forty-five (45) days' written notice to the other; until then it remains in force.

6.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and continue indefinitely, until the Confidential Information falls within one of the exclusions in this Agreement.

6.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 7. Indemnification

7.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

7.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 10. Return or Destruction

10.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

10.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 11. No Licence

11.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 12. Miscellaneous

12.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

12.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 13. No Representations

13.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

13.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 14. Use of Confidential Information

14.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 15. Confidentiality Obligations

15.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 16. Disclosure to Representatives

16.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

16.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 17. Information Not Covered

17.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

17.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.
