# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Brackenfield Foods Limited ("Brackenfield") and Northcote Logistics Inc. ("Northcote"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement is dated as of the twenty-first day of January, two thousand and twenty-six (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Brackenfield and Northcote are discussing a possible cold-chain distribution partnership (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Brackenfield and Northcote will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by inspection, and whether or not marked as confidential, including business plans, financial information, technical data, software, know-how, customer information and trade secrets.

2.2 Confidential Information also includes all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent that they contain or reflect any information described above.

## 3. General

3.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all earlier understandings about that subject matter. It may be amended only in writing signed by both of them.

3.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 5. Equitable Relief

5.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

5.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 6. Exclusions

6.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 7. No Warranty

7.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 8. Standard of Care

8.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

8.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 9. Return or Destruction

9.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

9.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 10. No Licence

10.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 11. No Solicitation of Staff

11.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 12. Indemnity

12.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.

13.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York.

13.3 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 14. Compelled Disclosure

14.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 15. Disclosure to Representatives

15.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

15.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 16. Term and Termination

16.1 This Agreement continues for thirty-six (36) months and then expires automatically.

16.2 Each obligation of confidentiality in this Agreement continues for twenty-four (24) months after this Agreement expires and then ends.

16.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 17. Permitted Purpose

17.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

17.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.
