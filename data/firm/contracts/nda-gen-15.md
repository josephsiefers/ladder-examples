# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Juniper Lane Robotics Pte. Ltd. ("Juniper Lane") and Marchetti Automation Ltd ("Marchetti"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on the date on which it has been signed by both parties, being the later of the dates of signature below (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Juniper Lane and Marchetti are discussing a possible distribution agreement for Juniper Lane's inspection drones (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Juniper Lane and Marchetti will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Information Not Covered

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

3.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 4. Liability

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

4.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

5.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 6. Standard of Care

6.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

6.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 7. Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

7.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Republic of Singapore.

7.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 8. No Solicitation of Staff

8.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 9. Miscellaneous

9.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

9.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 10. Equitable Relief

10.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

10.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 11. Representatives

11.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 12. Indemnity

12.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 13. Permitted Purpose

13.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

13.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 14. Duration

14.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of twelve (12) months from the date on which it takes effect, unless terminated earlier by agreement in writing.

14.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for twenty-four (24) months.

14.3 In respect of any Confidential Information that is a trade secret, those obligations continue indefinitely, for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

14.4 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 15. No Warranty

15.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 16. Intellectual Property

16.1 The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information other than the right to use it for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

## 17. Return or Destruction

17.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

17.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.
