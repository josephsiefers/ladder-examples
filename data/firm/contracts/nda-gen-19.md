# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Kittering Systems Limited ("Kittering") and Vossberg Cloud Services Inc. ("Vossberg"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement comes into force on the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Kittering and Vossberg wish to exchange information for a possible migration of Kittering's billing platform to Vossberg's hosting (the "Purpose"). Each of them may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and in this Agreement the "Disclosing Party" is the party disclosing Confidential Information and the "Receiving Party" is the party receiving it.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Use of Confidential Information

3.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 4. Equitable Relief

4.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

4.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 5. Liability

5.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

5.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 6. Representatives

6.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 7. Non-Solicitation

7.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 8. No Warranty

8.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 9. Return or Destruction

9.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

9.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 10. Exclusions

10.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 11. No Licence

11.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of three (3) years from the date on which it takes effect, unless terminated earlier by agreement in writing.

12.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and continue indefinitely, until the Confidential Information falls within one of the exclusions in this Agreement.

12.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 13. Compelled Disclosure

13.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 14. Miscellaneous

14.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

14.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 15. Indemnity

15.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 16. Law and Jurisdiction

16.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

16.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

16.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 17. Confidentiality Obligations

17.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.
