# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 September 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Halden Robotics Pte. Ltd. and its Affiliates, a company incorporated in Singapore with unique entity number 201912345K and its registered office at 71 Science Park Drive, Singapore 118254 ("Halden"), and Corvane Systems Limited, a corporation incorporated under the laws of the Province of Ontario with its head office at 250 Front Street West, Toronto, Ontario M5V 3G5 ("Corvane"). Halden and Corvane are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1.2 The Parties intend to discuss a possible arrangement under which Corvane's machine-vision software would be deployed on Halden's warehouse robots (the "Project").

1.3 Each Party will disclose Confidential Information to the other in connection with the Project, and each Party agrees to keep the other Party's Confidential Information confidential on the terms below.

## 2. Interpretation

2.1 "Affiliate" means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party.

2.2 "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature disclosed by a Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient") in connection with the Project, whether in writing, orally or by any other means, including trade secrets, technical specifications, source code, product designs, business plans and commercial terms.

2.3 "Trade Secret" means any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 The Recipient shall keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential, shall use it only for the Project, and shall not disclose it to any person other than its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know it for the Project and are bound by duties of confidentiality.

3.2 The Recipient shall protect the Discloser's Confidential Information with the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in any case with no less than reasonable care.

## 4. Exceptions

4.1 Clause 3 does not apply to information that: (a) is in or enters the public domain other than through the Recipient's breach; (b) the Recipient already lawfully possessed without restriction; (c) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction; or (d) the Recipient independently develops without reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

4.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information where required by law or by any court or regulatory authority, giving the Discloser prior notice where permitted.

## 5. Duration

5.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues in force for a period of three (3) years.

5.2 Following the expiry or termination of this Agreement, the obligations in clause 3 shall continue to apply to Confidential Information for a further two (2) years.

5.3 Notwithstanding clause 5.2, the obligations in clause 3 shall continue to apply to any Trade Secret for so long as that information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 6. Return of Information

6.1 On expiry or termination of this Agreement, or on the Discloser's earlier request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies that it is required by law to retain.

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser, and no right or licence is granted to the Recipient except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning its subject matter. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

8.2 Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

8.3 A person who is not a Party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 of Singapore to enforce any term of this Agreement.

## 9. Governing Law and Disputes

9.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

9.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Republic of Singapore in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
