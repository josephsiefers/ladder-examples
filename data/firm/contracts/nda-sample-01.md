# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the third day of March, two thousand and twenty-five (the "Effective Date") between Kestrel Bioanalytics Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 08812345 and its registered office at 14 Wharf Road, Cambridge CB2 1AB ("Kestrel"), and Marlow Data Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11923456 and its registered office at 3 Abbey Lane, Reading RG1 4PQ ("Marlow"). Kestrel and Marlow are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1.2 The Parties wish to explore a possible collaboration in which Marlow's laboratory data platform would be integrated with Kestrel's sample-analysis services (the "Purpose").

1.3 In the course of their discussions about the Purpose, each Party expects to disclose Confidential Information to the other. Each Party is therefore both a disclosing party (a "Discloser") and a receiving party (a "Recipient") under this Agreement, and the Parties have agreed to protect each other's Confidential Information on the terms set out below.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information, in any form or medium, that is disclosed by or on behalf of a Discloser to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, whether before, on or after the Effective Date, and that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of its disclosure. It includes, without limitation, technical data, software, algorithms, laboratory methods, product plans, pricing, customer and supplier information, business plans and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions.

2.2 "Representatives" means a Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 3. Obligations of Confidentiality

3.1 The Recipient shall keep the Discloser's Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall not disclose it to any person except its Representatives in accordance with clause 3.3, or as permitted by clause 5.

3.2 The Recipient shall use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose and for no other purpose, and shall protect it with at least the same degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event with no less than reasonable care.

3.3 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives, provided that it informs them of the confidential nature of the information before disclosure, ensures that they are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement, and remains responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if done by the Recipient.

3.4 The Recipient shall not copy, reduce to writing or otherwise record any Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose, and any such copies shall remain the property of the Discloser.

3.5 The Recipient shall promptly notify the Discloser in writing on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of the Discloser's Confidential Information and shall take all reasonable steps to limit the effect of that use or disclosure.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 The obligations in clause 3 do not apply to information that the Recipient can show by written records: (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any obligation of confidence before it was disclosed by the Discloser; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is not under an obligation of confidence in respect of it; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

5.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, by any governmental or regulatory authority, or by a court of competent jurisdiction, provided that, to the extent it is legally permitted to do so, it gives the Discloser as much notice of the disclosure as possible and cooperates with the Discloser, at the Discloser's expense, in any lawful attempt to limit the disclosure or obtain a protective order.

## 6. Return and Destruction

6.1 On the Discloser's written request at any time, and in any event on the expiry or termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing the Discloser's Confidential Information, erase it from its computer systems so far as reasonably practicable, and confirm in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Recipient may retain Confidential Information to the extent required by law or held in routine electronic back-up systems, provided that the retained information remains subject to the obligations of this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

## 7. Term and Survival

7.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force for a period of twenty-four (24) months, after which it shall expire automatically unless the Parties agree in writing to extend it.

7.2 Notwithstanding the expiry or termination of this Agreement, the obligations of confidentiality and restricted use in clause 3 shall survive and continue to bind each Recipient for a period of five (5) years from the date of expiry or termination.

## 8. No Licence and No Warranty

8.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence or other right in respect of any Confidential Information, patent, copyright, trade mark or other intellectual property right of the Discloser, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

8.2 Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Discloser gives no warranty, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose, and shall have no liability to the Recipient arising from its use, except in the case of fraud.

## 9. No Obligation to Proceed

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any particular information, to continue discussions about the Purpose, or to enter into any further agreement. Either Party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time by written notice to the other.

## 10. Remedies

10.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement. Accordingly, without prejudice to any other rights and remedies it may have, the Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach of this Agreement.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, arrangements and agreements between them relating to that subject matter.

11.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by an authorised signatory of each Party.

11.3 Neither Party may assign, transfer or otherwise deal with any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

11.4 A person who is not a Party to this Agreement shall have no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any of its terms.

11.5 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

11.6 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one agreement.

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim, including any non-contractual dispute or claim, arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

12.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation.
