# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Albright Water Technologies Limited ("Albright") and Calder Infrastructure Partners LLP ("Calder"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement is dated as of the twenty-second day of October, two thousand and twenty-five (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Albright and Calder have engaged in mutual discussions concerning a possible acquisition of Albright by Calder (the "Purpose").

1.4 To allow those discussions to continue, Albright (the "Disclosing Party") is prepared to make Confidential Information available to Calder (the "Receiving Party"). Calder will not provide confidential information under this Agreement, and only Calder is bound by obligations of confidentiality.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Miscellaneous

3.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

3.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 6. Permitted Purpose

6.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

6.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

7.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

7.3 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 8. Return of Materials

8.1 Upon the expiry or termination of this Agreement, or earlier at the request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and shall certify the destruction in writing if the Disclosing Party asks it to.

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 10. Standard of Care

10.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

10.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 11. Representatives

11.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 12. No Warranty

12.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 13. Intellectual Property

13.1 The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information other than the right to use it for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

## 14. Indemnification

14.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

14.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 15. Remedies

15.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 16. Duration

16.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice to the other; until then it remains in force.

16.2 The obligations in this Agreement in respect of Confidential Information disclosed before its expiry or termination continue for a further twenty-four (24) months after it ends.

16.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 17. Legally Required Disclosure

17.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

17.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.
