# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Riverglass Media Inc. ("Riverglass") and Fennimore Studios LLC and its Affiliates ("Fennimore").

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on April 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Riverglass and Fennimore are discussing a possible co-production of a documentary series (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Riverglass and Fennimore will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Indemnity

3.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 4. No Solicitation of Staff

4.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 5. Remedies

5.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 6. Return or Destruction

6.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Legally Required Disclosure

7.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

7.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 8. Duration

8.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of two (2) years from the date on which it takes effect, unless terminated earlier by agreement in writing.

8.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality shall remain in force for a period of three (3) years from the date on which this Agreement expires or terminates.

8.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 9. No Licence

9.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

10.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 11. Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York.

11.2 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 12. Confidentiality Obligations

12.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 13. Use of Confidential Information

13.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 14. No Warranty

14.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 15. Exclusions

15.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 16. Disclosure to Representatives

16.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

16.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 17. Miscellaneous

17.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

17.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.
