# Confidentiality Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Confidentiality Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Pemberton Aerospace Limited and its Affiliates ("Pemberton") and Larkspur Machining LLC ("Larkspur"), each a "party".

1.2 Pemberton and Larkspur have engaged in mutual discussions concerning a possible supply of Larkspur's precision components to Pemberton (the "Purpose").

1.3 To allow those discussions to continue, Larkspur (the "Disclosing Party") is prepared to make Confidential Information available to Pemberton (the "Receiving Party"). Pemberton will not provide confidential information under this Agreement, and only Pemberton is bound by obligations of confidentiality.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Intellectual Property

3.1 The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information other than the right to use it for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1 This Agreement continues for twenty-four (24) months and then expires automatically.

4.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for twelve (12) months.

4.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 5. Disclosure to Representatives

5.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

5.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 6. Permitted Purpose

6.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

6.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

8.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 9. Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York.

9.2 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 10. Return or Destruction

10.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

10.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 11. Remedies

11.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 12. Legally Required Disclosure

12.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

12.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 13. Confidentiality Obligations

13.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 14. Exclusions

14.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 15. Non-Solicitation

15.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 16. No Representations

16.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

16.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 17. General

17.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all earlier understandings about that subject matter. It may be amended only in writing signed by both of them.

17.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original.
