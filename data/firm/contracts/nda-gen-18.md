# Confidentiality Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Confidentiality Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Delphine Cosmetics Inc. ("Delphine") and Orchard Row Retail LLC ("Orchard Row"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement is made on the 1st day of December 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Delphine (the "Disclosing Party") intends to disclose Confidential Information to Orchard Row (the "Receiving Party") for a possible exclusive retail launch of Delphine's skincare range (the "Purpose"). Orchard Row will not disclose confidential information under this Agreement, which imposes obligations of confidentiality on Orchard Row only.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Legally Required Disclosure

3.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

3.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 4. General

4.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all earlier understandings about that subject matter. It may be amended only in writing signed by both of them.

4.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original.

## 5. Use of Confidential Information

5.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 6. Indemnification

6.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

6.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information other than the right to use it for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

## 8. Return or Destruction

8.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

8.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 9. Confidentiality Obligations

9.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 10. Equitable Relief

10.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

10.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 11. Duration

11.1 This Agreement continues for eighteen (18) months and then expires automatically.

11.2 Each obligation of confidentiality in this Agreement continues for thirty (30) months after this Agreement expires and then ends.

11.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 12. Representatives

12.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 13. No Solicitation of Staff

13.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 14. Information Not Covered

14.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

14.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 15. Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of New York.

15.2 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 16. No Representations

16.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

16.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 17. Limitation of Liability

17.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.
