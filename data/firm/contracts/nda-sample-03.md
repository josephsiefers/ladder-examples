# Confidentiality Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Confidentiality Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of July 14, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and is made between Harrow Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal office at 88 Hudson Yards, New York, New York 10001 (the "Disclosing Party"), and Pellucid Capital Partners LLC, a Delaware limited liability company having its principal office at 1201 North Market Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (the "Recipient").

1.2 The Disclosing Party and the Recipient have engaged in mutual discussions concerning a possible minority investment by the Recipient in the Disclosing Party (the "Transaction").

1.3 To allow the Recipient to evaluate the Transaction, the Disclosing Party is prepared to make available to the Recipient certain information about its business, clinical programmes and finances. The Recipient will not provide confidential information to the Disclosing Party under this Agreement, and this Agreement imposes obligations of confidentiality on the Recipient only.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Evaluation Material" means all information concerning the Disclosing Party or its business, whether furnished before or after the Effective Date and in whatever form, that is furnished to the Recipient or its Representatives by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party, together with all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Recipient or its Representatives that contain or reflect such information.

2.2 "Representatives" means the Recipient's affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, partners, legal counsel, accountants and financial advisers.

2.3 Evaluation Material does not include information that: (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Recipient or its Representatives; (b) was available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party; or (c) becomes available to the Recipient on a non-confidential basis from a source other than the Disclosing Party that is not prohibited from disclosing it.

## 3. Use and Non-Disclosure

3.1 The Recipient shall, and shall direct its Representatives to, keep the Evaluation Material strictly confidential and use it solely for the purpose of evaluating, negotiating and consummating the Transaction.

3.2 The Recipient shall not disclose any Evaluation Material, or the fact that discussions concerning the Transaction are taking place, to any person other than those of its Representatives who need to know it for the purpose described in clause 3.1 and who are informed of its confidential nature.

3.3 The Recipient shall be liable for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives.

## 4. Legally Required Disclosure

4.1 If the Recipient or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation or legal process to disclose any Evaluation Material, the Recipient shall, to the extent legally permitted, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order, and shall disclose only that portion of the Evaluation Material that it is legally required to disclose.

## 5. Return or Destruction

5.1 At any time upon the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all Evaluation Material and confirm the destruction in writing, save that the Recipient may retain copies to the extent required by applicable law or bona fide internal document-retention policies.

## 6. Term

6.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date.

6.2 The Recipient's obligations under clauses 3 and 5 shall continue for a period of eighteen (18) months following the expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement.

## 7. No Representations; No Obligation

7.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Evaluation Material. Only those representations and warranties made in a definitive agreement regarding the Transaction, when and if executed, shall have any legal effect.

7.2 Neither party shall be under any legal obligation with respect to the Transaction unless and until a definitive agreement has been executed and delivered.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 For a period of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, the Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Transaction, provided that general solicitations not directed at such persons shall not breach this clause.

## 9. Remedies

9.1 The Recipient acknowledges that money damages would not be a sufficient remedy for any breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief as remedies for any such breach, without proof of actual damages.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of New York, without regard to the conflict of laws principles thereof.

10.2 Each party irrevocably and unconditionally submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to the laying of venue in those courts.

10.3 This Agreement contains the entire agreement between the parties concerning the confidentiality of the Evaluation Material. No waiver or modification of this Agreement shall be binding unless in writing and signed by both parties.

10.4 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
