# Letter of Intent: Proposed Acquisition of the Ashby Bakery Business

Private and confidential. Subject to contract.

Dear Mr and Mrs Ashby,

Further to our recent meetings, we are pleased to set out the principal terms on which Greystone Foods Ltd ("Greystone") would be willing to acquire the entire issued share capital of Ashby Bakery Limited (the "Company"). Except where stated below, this letter is not intended to be legally binding and records only our present intentions.

1. Price. Greystone proposes to pay £4,200,000 for the shares on a cash-free, debt-free basis, assuming a normal level of working capital. Of the price, £3,600,000 would be paid on completion and £600,000 would be deferred for twelve months and paid subject to the Company achieving revenue of at least £5,000,000 in the year following completion.

2. Due diligence. Our offer is subject to satisfactory financial, legal, commercial and environmental due diligence. We would expect to complete due diligence within six weeks of your acceptance of this letter, and we would ask you to give our advisers reasonable access to the Company's records, premises and senior staff for that purpose.

3. Employees. We value the Company's staff and its reputation in the region. We intend to keep the bakery at Mill Street operating and to retain all current employees on their existing terms. We would like Mrs Ashby to stay on as managing director for a transitional period of at least eighteen months, on terms to be agreed.

4. Conditions. Completion would be conditional on the signing of a share purchase agreement containing warranties and indemnities customary for a transaction of this kind, the approval of Greystone's board, and the consent of the Company's landlord to the change of control under the Mill Street lease.

5. Exclusivity. In return for Greystone committing time and cost to due diligence, you agree that for eight weeks from the date you countersign this letter neither you nor the Company will solicit, encourage or enter into discussions with any other person about the sale of the Company or its business. This paragraph is legally binding.

6. Confidentiality. Each of us will keep the existence and terms of this letter, and any information received from the other in connection with the proposed acquisition, confidential, and will not disclose it to anyone other than our professional advisers and financiers, except where required by law. This paragraph is legally binding and will continue to apply for two years whether or not the acquisition proceeds.

7. Costs. Each party will bear its own costs in connection with the proposed acquisition, whether or not it completes.

8. Governing law. This letter, and any dispute arising from it, is governed by the law of England and Wales.

We look forward to working with you. If you are content with these terms, please sign and return a copy of this letter.

Yours sincerely,

Marcus Lyle, Finance Director, for and on behalf of Greystone Foods Ltd
