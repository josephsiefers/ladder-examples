# Lease of Commercial Premises

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 Harrow Analytics Ltd ("Harrow") grants Belgrave Retail Group plc ("Belgrave") a lease of the warehouse and yard at 18 Mill Lane (the "Property").

1.2 The lease is for a term of five years for use as a storage and distribution warehouse, and is granted on the terms of this document.

## 2. Rent

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a yearly rent of GBP 84,000, exclusive of VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days.

## 3. Break and Assignment

3.1 The Tenant may not assign this lease without the Landlord's prior written consent. Either party may end the lease at the end of year three on six months' notice.

## 4. Repair and Service Charge

4.1 The Tenant is responsible for repairs to the Property other than the roof and structure, which the Landlord shall maintain.

## 5. Use and Alterations

5.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices. The Tenant shall not make structural alterations, and shall not make other alterations without the Landlord's consent, which is not to be unreasonably withheld.
