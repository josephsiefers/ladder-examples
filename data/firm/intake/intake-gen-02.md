# Framework Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Fennick Logistics plc ("Fennick" or the "Customer") and Corvane Systems Limited ("Corvane" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Fennick wishes to appoint Corvane as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 Either party may terminate this agreement or any statement of work immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty days of being notified of it.

## 3. Liability

3.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all statements of work.

## 4. Services

4.1 The Supplier shall provide the services in accordance with each statement of work and any service levels it sets out. If the Supplier expects to miss a milestone it shall notify the Customer promptly and propose a recovery plan.

## 5. Change Control

5.1 Either party may request a change to a statement of work. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change note stating its effect on scope, timetable and fees.

## 6. Fees and Payment

6.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rates in the relevant statement of work, unless it states a fixed price.

6.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index, on sixty days' written notice.
