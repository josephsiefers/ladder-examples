# Master Services and Consulting Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Fennick Logistics plc ("Fennick" or the "Customer") and Corvane Systems Limited ("Corvane" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Fennick wishes to appoint Corvane as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Statements of Work

2.1 The Customer may request services at any time by sending the Supplier a draft statement of work. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days, either accepting the draft or proposing changes to it, and no statement of work takes effect until signed by an authorised representative of each party.

2.2 Where a statement of work and these master terms are inconsistent, these master terms take precedence.

## 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the services in accordance with each statement of work and any service levels it sets out, and shall comply with the Customer's reasonable site rules when working at the Customer's premises.

3.2 If the Supplier expects to miss a date set out in a statement of work, it shall notify the Customer promptly, explain the cause, and propose a plan to recover the delay.

## 4. Personnel

4.1 The Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its personnel and subcontractors. It may subcontract services only with the Customer's prior written consent, and shall remove from the services any individual the Customer reasonably objects to.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rates in the relevant statement of work, unless it states a fixed price. Invoices are payable within thirty days of receipt.

5.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index, on sixty days' written notice. An increase does not apply to a fixed price already agreed.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each party keeps the intellectual property it owned before a statement of work began. Deliverables created specifically for the Customer under a statement of work belong to the Customer once it has paid for them, and the Supplier grants the Customer a perpetual licence to use any of the Supplier's pre-existing materials included in them.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all statements of work, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Termination of this agreement ends every statement of work then in force unless the parties agree otherwise.

8.2 Either party may terminate this agreement or any statement of work immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty days of being notified of it.

## 9. General

9.1 Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent. Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses stated in the relevant statement of work. This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
