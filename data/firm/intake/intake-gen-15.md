# Framework Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Brightwell Clinics Ltd ("Brightwell" or the "Customer") and Haldane Capital Partners LLP ("Haldane" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Brightwell wishes to appoint Haldane as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Termination of this agreement ends every statement of work then in force unless the parties agree otherwise.

## 3. Change Control

3.1 The Customer may ask for changes to the services by written change request. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days with the impact of the change, and shall implement it only once the Customer accepts that impact in writing.

## 4. Liability

4.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit. Each party's aggregate liability under this agreement is capped at one hundred and twenty-five per cent of the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in each statement of work. Unless a statement of work states otherwise, the Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears and the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt.

## 6. Services

6.1 The Supplier shall provide the services in accordance with each statement of work and any service levels it sets out. If the Supplier expects to miss a milestone it shall notify the Customer promptly and propose a recovery plan.
