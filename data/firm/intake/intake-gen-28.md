# Data Processing Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This addendum is entered into by Haldane Capital Partners LLP ("Haldane") and its customer Brightwell Clinics Ltd ("Brightwell").

1.2 Haldane processes personal data as a processor for Brightwell, which is the controller of that data. This addendum governs that processing: the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1.

## 2. Processing on Instructions

2.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required to do so by law, in which case it shall inform the Controller of that requirement first.

## 3. Sub-processors

3.1 The Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in Annex 3. The Processor shall impose on each sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this agreement, and remains liable for their performance.

## 4. Security and Personnel

4.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the personal data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and regular testing.

4.2 The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

## 5. Data Subject Rights and Deletion

5.1 The Processor shall promptly pass to the Controller any request it receives from a data subject and shall not respond to it except on the Controller's instructions. Within sixty days after the services end, the Processor shall delete all personal data and certify that it has done so.

## 6. Personal Data Breaches

6.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach, and shall give the Controller the information it needs to meet its own reporting obligations.
