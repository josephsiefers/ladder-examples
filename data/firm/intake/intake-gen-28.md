# Employment Contract

## 1. Parties and Appointment

1.1 This agreement is made between Bramcote Analytics Limited (the "Company") and Daniel Okafor (the "Employee").

1.2 The Company employs the Employee as Head of Finance Operations, reporting to the Chief Financial Officer, on the terms of this agreement. The employment starts on 4 January 2027, and no period of previous employment counts towards the Employee's period of continuous employment. This agreement contains the written statement of particulars of employment that the Employment Rights Act 1996 requires.

## 2. Place of Work and Hours

2.1 The Employee shall work 37.5 hours a week, Monday to Friday, and such additional hours as are needed to perform the role properly, for which no overtime is paid. The Employee's place of work is the Company's Leeds office, and the Employee may be asked to travel to the Company's other sites in the United Kingdom.

## 3. Salary and Benefits

3.1 The Employee's basic salary is £78,000 a year, which accrues from day to day and is payable in equal monthly instalments. The Company reviews salaries every April. The Employee is also eligible for the Company's annual bonus scheme, up to fifteen per cent of salary, at the Company's discretion and subject to the scheme rules.

## 4. Pension

4.1 The Employee will be auto-enrolled into the Company's group personal pension plan. The Company shall pay contributions equal to six per cent of basic salary, provided the Employee contributes at least four per cent. Further details are in the scheme booklet, which the Company has given the Employee.

## 5. Holidays and Sickness

5.1 The Employee is entitled to twenty-five working days' paid holiday in each holiday year, which runs from 1 January, in addition to the usual public holidays in England. Holiday must be agreed in advance with the Chief Financial Officer.

5.2 If the Employee is absent through sickness, the Employee shall tell the Company by 10.00 am on the first day. The Company pays full salary for up to ten weeks' sickness absence in any twelve months, and statutory sick pay after that.

## 6. Duties and Conduct

6.1 During the employment the Employee shall devote the whole of their working time to the Company's business, and shall not take up other paid work without the Company's written consent.

6.2 The Employee shall comply with the Company's policies, including its staff handbook, as amended from time to time. Those policies are not contractual.

## 7. Probation and Notice

7.1 The employment is subject to a six-month probationary period, which the Company may extend by up to three months, and one week's notice applies on either side during it. Once probation is complete, the Employee shall give three months' notice to resign, and the Company shall give three months' notice, or pay in lieu of notice, to terminate the employment.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall treat as confidential all information it receives from the other about the other's business, customers, finances, staff and technology ("Confidential Information"), whether disclosed before or after the date of this agreement.

8.2 Neither party shall use the other's Confidential Information except for the purposes of this agreement, nor disclose it to any person other than its own officers, employees and professional advisers who need to know it for those purposes and who are told of its confidential nature.

8.3 This clause does not cover information already lawfully in the public domain, or a disclosure required by law, and it survives the end of this agreement for three years.

## 9. Termination and Company Property

9.1 The Company may terminate the employment with immediate effect if the Employee is guilty of gross misconduct, is convicted of a criminal offence other than a minor road traffic offence, or becomes bankrupt. On termination for any reason, the Employee shall return all property belonging to the Company.
