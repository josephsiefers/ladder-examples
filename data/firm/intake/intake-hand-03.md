# Data Processing Agreement

Sent by Orla Brennan, Privacy Lead, Tamsin Health Ltd: "Our payroll provider has sent this over to sit alongside the existing services contract. Please review before we countersign. They process employee data only."

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement is made on 10 November 2026 between Tamsin Health Ltd, registered in England and Wales with number 07733218 (the "Controller"), and Payline Bureau Services Limited, registered in England and Wales with number 08856104 (the "Processor").

1.2 The parties have entered into a payroll services agreement dated 2 March 2024 (the "Services Agreement"). In providing those services the Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. This agreement sets out the terms of that processing as required by Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

## 2. Details of Processing

2.1 Subject matter and duration: the provision of payroll services for the term of the Services Agreement.

2.2 Nature and purpose: calculation and payment of salaries, pension contributions and statutory deductions, and the production of payslips and reports for HMRC.

2.3 Categories of data subjects: current and former employees of the Controller and their nominated beneficiaries.

2.4 Types of personal data: names, addresses, dates of birth, National Insurance numbers, bank details, salary and tax information, and limited health data where it affects statutory sick pay.

## 3. Processor Obligations

3.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers outside the United Kingdom, unless required to do so by law, in which case it shall inform the Controller before processing unless the law prohibits this.

3.2 The Processor shall ensure that all persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including encryption of personal data at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, and regular testing of those measures.

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 The Processor shall not engage another processor without the prior written authorisation of the Controller. The Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in Schedule 1. The Processor shall give the Controller at least thirty days' notice of any intended addition or replacement, during which the Controller may object on reasonable grounds.

4.2 The Processor shall impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than those in this agreement and remains fully liable to the Controller for the performance of each sub-processor.

## 5. Assistance and Breach Notification

5.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights, and with data protection impact assessments and consultations with the Information Commissioner.

5.2 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach, giving the information the Controller needs to meet its own reporting obligations.

## 6. International Transfers

6.1 The Processor shall not transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom without the Controller's prior written consent and, where consent is given, only under the International Data Transfer Agreement or another lawful transfer mechanism.

## 7. Audit, Deletion and Return

7.1 The Processor shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement and allow for audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor it appoints, on reasonable notice.

7.2 On termination of the Services Agreement, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data and delete existing copies unless the law requires their storage.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
