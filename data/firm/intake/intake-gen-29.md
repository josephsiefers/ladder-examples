# Lease

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Lease is made between Corvane Systems Limited (the "Landlord") and Fennick Logistics plc (the "Tenant").

1.2 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the second floor of Unit 4, Harbour Business Park (the "Premises") for a term of ten years from the start date, at the rent and on the terms set out below.

## 2. Repair and Service Charge

2.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair and decorate it in the last year of the term. The Tenant shall pay a fair proportion of the cost of maintaining the common parts as a service charge.

## 3. Use and Alterations

3.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices. The Tenant shall not make structural alterations, and shall not make other alterations without the Landlord's consent, which is not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 4. Rent

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a yearly rent of GBP 84,000, exclusive of VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days.

## 5. Break and Assignment

5.1 The Tenant may not assign this lease without the Landlord's prior written consent. Either party may end the lease at the end of year three on six months' notice.
