# Data Protection Agreement

## 1. Parties and Purpose

1.1 This agreement is made between Lyndale Cloud Limited (the "Controller") and Thornbury Health Ltd (the "Processor").

1.2 The Processor provides hosted payroll services to the Controller under a separate services agreement, and in doing so processes the personal data of the Controller's employees on the Controller's behalf. This agreement sets out the terms on which the Processor processes that personal data, as Article 28 of the UK GDPR requires, and it applies for as long as the Processor holds any of it.

## 2. Details of the Processing

2.1 The Processor processes personal data only to provide the payroll services: calculating pay, deductions and pension contributions, producing payslips and making returns to HM Revenue & Customs. The data subjects are the Controller's current and former employees. The personal data comprises names, contact details, National Insurance numbers, bank details, salary and tax information, and records of absence.

## 3. Processor's Obligations

3.1 The Processor shall process the personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, including those in this agreement, unless the law requires otherwise, in which case it shall tell the Controller before processing unless the law forbids it. The Processor shall tell the Controller at once if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.

3.2 The Processor shall ensure that every person it authorises to process the personal data is bound by a duty of confidentiality.

## 4. Security

4.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing and the risks to employees, the Processor shall keep the personal data secure. It shall encrypt the data, restrict access to staff who need it for the payroll services, keep logs of that access, and maintain a certified information security management system for the whole of the processing.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Controller gives the Processor general authorisation to use sub-processors, provided the Processor keeps an up-to-date list of them, tells the Controller of any intended addition or replacement at least thirty days in advance, and binds each one by written contract to the same data protection obligations as this agreement. The Processor is liable for the acts of every sub-processor it uses.

## 6. International Transfers

6.1 The Processor shall not transfer the personal data outside the United Kingdom, or allow a sub-processor to do so, without the Controller's prior written consent. Any transfer the Controller consents to shall be made under the international data transfer agreement issued by the Information Commissioner, or another safeguard that data protection law recognises.

## 7. Personal Data Breaches

7.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach. The notice shall describe the breach, the categories and approximate number of employees affected, its likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to deal with it.

7.2 The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller's investigation and help it meet its obligations to report the breach to the Information Commissioner and to tell affected employees.

## 8. Assistance and Audit

8.1 The Processor shall pass to the Controller, within five working days, any request it receives from an employee to access, correct or erase their personal data, and shall not answer it except on the Controller's instructions. Once a year, or after a personal data breach, the Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this agreement on fourteen days' notice.

## 9. Return and Deletion

9.1 On termination or expiry of the services agreement, the Processor shall stop processing the personal data, return it to the Controller in the format the Controller reasonably asks for, and then securely delete all copies, certifying in writing that it has done so. The Processor may keep only what the law requires it to keep, and shall keep that confidential.
