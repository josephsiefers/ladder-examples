# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Ashgrove Robotics Limited ("Ashgrove") and Penhallow Data Ltd ("Penhallow").

1.2 This Agreement is dated 3 March 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Ashgrove and Penhallow wish to exchange information for a possible joint development of warehouse picking robots (the "Purpose"). Each of them may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and in this Agreement the "Disclosing Party" is the party disclosing Confidential Information and the "Receiving Party" is the party receiving it.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Return or Destruction

3.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

3.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 4. Permitted Purpose

4.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 5. No Solicitation of Staff

5.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 6. Compelled Disclosure

6.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 7. Representatives

7.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 8. Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

8.2 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 9. General

9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and supersedes all earlier understandings about that subject matter. It may be amended only in writing signed by both of them.

9.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original.

## 10. Standard of Care

10.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

10.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 11. Remedies

11.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

12.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 13. No Representations

13.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

13.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 14. Duration

14.1 This Agreement continues for two (2) years and then expires automatically.

14.2 Each obligation of confidentiality in this Agreement continues for five (5) years after this Agreement expires and then ends.

14.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 15. Exclusions

15.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 16. Intellectual Property

16.1 The Disclosing Party reserves all rights in its Confidential Information. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Confidential Information other than the right to use it for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

## 17. Indemnification

17.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

17.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.
