# Contract of Employment

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 Saltmarsh Media Limited ("Saltmarsh") offers, and Daniel Okafor accepts, employment as Senior Data Engineer on the terms of this agreement.

1.2 The employment begins on 6 January 2027. The first six months are a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment on two weeks' notice.

## 2. Pay and Benefits

2.1 The salary is GBP 76,500 a year, reviewed each April. The Employee is entitled to private medical insurance and life cover at four times salary.

## 3. Duties and Place of Work

3.1 The Employee shall devote their full working time to the business and shall not take other paid work without written consent. The Employee works from the Bristol office.

## 4. Hours and Holiday

4.1 The Employee works thirty-seven and a half hours a week and is entitled to twenty-five days' holiday plus public holidays. Up to five unused days may be carried forward.

## 5. Notice and Termination

5.1 After probation, either party may end the employment by giving three months' written notice. The Company may pay salary in lieu of notice.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party may disclose confidential information to the other under this agreement. "Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by a party, including its business plans, pricing, software, data and know-how.

6.2 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform this agreement, and disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

6.3 These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the receiving party, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law. They survive termination of this agreement for five years.
