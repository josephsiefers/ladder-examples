# Framework Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is made between Saltire Data Services Ltd (the "Supplier") and Ivybridge Insurance plc (the "Customer").

1.2 The Supplier provides technology consulting, implementation and support services. This agreement sets out the master terms on which the Supplier will provide services to the Customer from time to time. Each engagement will be described in a statement of work signed by both parties, and each statement of work forms a separate contract incorporating these terms.

## 2. Statements of Work

2.1 The Customer may request services at any time by sending the Supplier a draft statement of work. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days, either accepting the draft or proposing changes to it, and no statement of work takes effect until signed by an authorised representative of each party.

2.2 Where a statement of work and these master terms are inconsistent, these master terms take precedence.

## 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall perform the services described in each statement of work with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and using appropriately qualified personnel.

3.2 The Customer shall give the Supplier reasonable access to its premises, systems and staff as the services require, and shall make decisions promptly where the Supplier needs them.

## 4. Personnel

4.1 The Supplier shall name a delivery manager for each statement of work, who shall be the Customer's day-to-day contact. The Supplier shall not replace the delivery manager, or any other key person named in a statement of work, without the Customer's prior consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in each statement of work. Unless a statement of work states otherwise, the Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears and the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt.

5.2 The Customer shall notify any disputed amount within fifteen days of receiving the invoice, giving its reasons, and the parties shall try in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 The Supplier owns its tools, methods and know-how, including any it improves while performing the services. On payment, the Customer owns the deliverables named in each statement of work, and receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence of any Supplier material incorporated in them, for its own business purposes.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit. Each party's aggregate liability under this agreement is capped at one hundred and twenty-five per cent of the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim, save for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This agreement starts on signature and continues until terminated. Either party may end it on ninety days' written notice, but a statement of work in force at that time continues on these terms until its services are complete.

8.2 The Customer may end a statement of work early on thirty days' notice, and shall then pay for the services performed up to the date of termination.

## 9. General

9.1 This agreement, together with each statement of work, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter. Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties. This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

## 10. Schedule: Data Processing Terms

10.1 This Schedule applies whenever the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer in providing the services. The Customer is the controller and the Supplier is the processor of that personal data, which concerns the Customer's employees and customers and consists of names, contact details and account records.

10.2 The Supplier shall process the personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, ensure that every person authorised to process it is bound by confidentiality, and keep it secure by appropriate technical and organisational measures.

10.3 The Supplier shall not engage a sub-processor without the Customer's prior written authorisation, shall not transfer the personal data outside the United Kingdom without appropriate safeguards, and shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight hours, of any personal data breach.

10.4 When the services end, the Supplier shall, at the Customer's choice, delete or return all the personal data, and shall make available to the Customer all information needed to show compliance with this Schedule, including by allowing audits.
