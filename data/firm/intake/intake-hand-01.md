# One-Way Confidentiality Agreement

From: Priya Raman, Head of Corporate Development, Halden Robotics Ltd
To: Legal intake

Please review the attached agreement before Thursday. Brightwater Capital Partners want to look at our books ahead of a possible minority investment, and they have asked us to use their form. Only we will be sharing information; they will not be sending us anything of substance.

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is made on 4 November 2026 between Halden Robotics Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with number 09812274, whose registered office is at 14 Canal Wharf, Leeds LS11 5PS (the "Discloser"), and Brightwater Capital Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales with number OC417730, whose registered office is at 3 Lombard Court, London EC3V 9AA (the "Recipient").

1.2 The Discloser intends to make available to the Recipient certain information about its business, finances, products and intellectual property so that the Recipient can evaluate a possible investment in the Discloser (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of the Discloser to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, whether before or after the date of this agreement, including financial statements, management accounts, forecasts, customer lists, source code, designs and the existence and status of the discussions between the parties.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public other than through a breach of this agreement, that was lawfully in the Recipient's possession before disclosure, that the Recipient receives from a third party who is free to disclose it, or that the Recipient develops independently without use of the Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of the Recipient

3.1 The Recipient shall keep the Confidential Information secret, shall use it only for the Purpose, and shall not disclose it to any person except its partners, employees and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by duties of confidentiality no less strict than those in this agreement.

3.2 The Recipient shall protect the Confidential Information with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

3.3 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, by a court or by any regulatory authority, provided that it gives the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful to do so and discloses only the part that is legally required.

## 4. Return of Information

4.1 On the Discloser's written request, or if the Recipient decides not to proceed with the investment, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and any notes or analyses containing it, and confirm in writing that it has done so, save for copies it must keep to comply with law or its internal compliance policies.

## 5. Term

5.1 This agreement continues for two years from its date. The Recipient's obligations under clause 3 survive for three years after it ends.

## 6. No Commitment

6.1 Nothing in this agreement obliges either party to proceed with any investment or other transaction, and no licence of any intellectual property is granted by it.

## 7. Remedies

7.1 The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this agreement and that the Discloser may seek an injunction or other equitable relief.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
