# Lease

## 1. Parties and Premises

1.1 This lease is made between Marlow Quay Estates Limited (the "Landlord") and Petrel Analytics Limited (the "Tenant").

1.2 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the whole of the second floor of Harcourt House, 14 Quayside Road, Bristol (the "Premises"), for a term of five years starting on 1 March 2027 (the "Term"). The Tenant may also use the entrance hall, lifts, stairs and toilets of the building in common with the Landlord's other tenants. The Premises are let as offices, on the terms of this lease.

## 2. Rent

2.1 The rent is £84,000 a year plus VAT, payable quarterly in advance by bank transfer. The rent for the first three months of the Term is waived to allow the Tenant to fit out the Premises.

2.2 The rent will be reviewed on the third anniversary of the start of the Term to the open market rent at that date, but it will never go down on review.

## 3. Service Charge and Outgoings

3.1 The Tenant shall pay a service charge of twenty per cent of the Landlord's costs of running the building, including repairs to its structure and common parts, security, cleaning and the building's insurance, by quarterly payments on account with the rent, adjusted when the year's accounts are prepared. The Tenant pays its own business rates, electricity, water and telephone charges.

## 4. Use

4.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices and for no other purpose. It shall not do anything at the Premises that causes a nuisance to the Landlord or to other occupiers of the building, or that may invalidate the building's insurance.

## 5. Repair and Alterations

5.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair and decorative condition, but need not put them in a better state than that shown in the schedule of condition the parties signed with this lease.

5.2 The Tenant shall make no structural alterations. It may install or move internal partitions with the Landlord's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. Assignment and Underletting

6.1 The Tenant may assign this lease, or underlet the whole of the Premises, only with the Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed. On an assignment, the Landlord may require the outgoing Tenant to guarantee the incoming tenant's obligations by an authorised guarantee agreement.

## 7. Break Option

7.1 The Tenant may end this lease on the third anniversary of the start of the Term by giving the Landlord at least six months' written notice, provided it has paid all rent due and gives up the Premises free of any occupiers on the break date.

## 8. Landlord's Obligations and Forfeiture

8.1 The Landlord shall insure the building against the usual risks and allow the Tenant quiet enjoyment of the Premises for the Term, so long as the Tenant pays the rent and observes this lease.

8.2 If the rent is unpaid for twenty-one days after it falls due, or the Tenant materially breaches this lease or becomes insolvent, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises and this lease shall end.

## 9. End of the Lease

9.1 When this lease ends, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises, return all keys and access cards, and remove any alterations the Landlord asks it to remove, making good the Premises afterwards. Before this lease was granted, the parties excluded the Tenant's right to a new lease under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954.
