# Statement of Work No. 3

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Statement of Work is made under the Master Services Agreement between Corvane Systems Limited (the "Supplier") and Fennick Logistics plc (the "Customer"), and incorporates its terms.

1.2 This Statement of Work orders one project: the migration of the Customer's warehouse inventory system to the Supplier's cloud platform. It sets out that project's scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria and fees.

## 2. Project Governance

2.1 A steering group of one senior representative of each party shall meet monthly and resolve any issue the project managers cannot.

## 3. Scope and Deliverables

3.1 Deliverables: a design document, a working portal with user login, order tracking and returns, integration with the Customer's order database, and a runbook for the Customer's support team.

## 4. Milestones

4.1 The project starts on the date both parties sign this Statement of Work. Design sign-off is due in week three, user acceptance testing begins in week nine and go-live is planned for week twelve.

## 5. Fees

5.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rate card in the master agreement, capped at USD 240,000. The Supplier shall report hours weekly and seek approval before exceeding eighty per cent of the cap.

## 6. Acceptance

6.1 The Customer shall test each deliverable within ten working days of delivery and either accept it or list the ways in which it fails the acceptance criteria. A deliverable not rejected within that period is treated as accepted.
