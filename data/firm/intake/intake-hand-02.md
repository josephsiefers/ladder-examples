# Master Services Agreement

Cover note from the client (Fennick Logistics plc): this is Corvane Systems' paper for the new warehouse software contract. Their account manager says the confidentiality section is "basically an NDA", so please check it carefully. Individual projects will follow under separate statements of work.

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Master Services Agreement is made on 18 November 2026 between Fennick Logistics plc, registered in England and Wales with number 04471982 (the "Customer"), and Corvane Systems Limited, registered in England and Wales with number 11290563 (the "Supplier").

1.2 The Supplier provides warehouse management software and related implementation, support and consulting services. The Customer wishes to engage the Supplier to provide such services from time to time on the terms of this agreement.

## 2. Structure

2.1 This agreement sets out the general terms on which the Supplier will provide services. Each specific engagement will be described in a statement of work signed by both parties ("SOW"). Each SOW forms a separate contract incorporating the terms of this agreement.

2.2 If there is a conflict between this agreement and a SOW, this agreement prevails unless the SOW expressly states that a named clause of this agreement is varied.

## 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall perform the services described in each SOW with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and using appropriately qualified personnel.

3.2 The Supplier shall meet any milestones set out in a SOW. If the Supplier expects to miss a milestone it shall notify the Customer promptly and propose a recovery plan.

3.3 The Customer shall provide the Supplier with reasonable access to its premises, systems and staff as needed for the services, and shall give timely decisions where the Supplier requires them.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in each SOW. Unless a SOW states otherwise, the Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears and the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt.

4.2 The Supplier may increase its day rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index over the previous twelve months, on sixty days' written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party may disclose confidential information to the other in connection with this agreement. "Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by a party, including its business plans, pricing, software, data and know-how.

5.2 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform or receive the services, and disclose it only to its employees, subcontractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

5.3 These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the receiving party, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law. They survive termination of this agreement for five years.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 The Supplier retains all intellectual property in its software and pre-existing materials. The Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use deliverables for its internal business purposes. Bespoke deliverables identified as such in a SOW belong to the Customer on payment.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all SOWs.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Either party may terminate it or any SOW immediately if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty days of notice.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
