# Re: Project Lantern: Confidentiality

Dear Calder & Finch,

1. Calder & Finch LLP ("Calder & Finch") is considering a possible acquisition of shares in Northcote Engineering Limited ("Northcote") and has asked for information about its business to evaluate it.

2. In return for Northcote making that information available, Calder & Finch agrees to keep it confidential on the terms below. These terms protect the information Northcote discloses; they do not oblige either party to proceed with any transaction.

3. Calder & Finch shall protect the Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and shall not copy it except as the evaluation requires.

4. Confidential Information includes financial information, customer lists, pricing, plans and know-how, and any notes or analyses Calder & Finch prepares that contain or reflect it.

5. If Calder & Finch decides not to proceed, it shall destroy all Confidential Information and confirm this in writing. The obligations continue for three years.

Please confirm your agreement by signing and returning a copy of this letter. Yours sincerely, Northcote Engineering Limited
