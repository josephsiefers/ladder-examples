# Letter Agreement: Confidentiality

## 1. Purpose of this Letter

1.1 Orrin Data Systems plc ("Orrin" or the "Discloser") writes to Vantage Ridge Equity LLP ("Vantage Ridge" or the "Recipient") to set out the confidentiality terms on which it will share information with the Recipient.

1.2 Vantage Ridge has asked for information about Orrin's logistics software division so that it can decide whether to make an offer to buy the division (the "Purpose"). In return for receiving that information, the Recipient undertakes to the Discloser to keep it confidential and to use it only for the Purpose, on the terms of this letter.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means any information, whether written, oral or electronic, that the Recipient receives from or on behalf of the Discloser in connection with the Purpose, including the division's accounts, customer lists, source code and employee data, and any analysis the Recipient prepares from it. Information already public, or lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure, is excluded.

## 3. Undertakings

3.1 The Recipient shall keep the Confidential Information secret, use it only for the Purpose, and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information. It shall not disclose it to anyone except as this letter allows. It shall keep a record of the people to whom it gives Confidential Information, and show that record to the Discloser on request.

## 4. Permitted Disclosure

4.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, employees and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose, provided it tells them it is confidential and remains responsible for their compliance with this letter.

4.2 The Recipient may also disclose Confidential Information if required by law or a regulator, after giving the Discloser as much notice as the law allows.

## 5. No Approach to Staff or Customers

5.1 While the Purpose is pursued, and for twelve months after, the Recipient shall not solicit any of the division's employees whom it met or learned of through the Confidential Information, and shall direct all enquiries about the division to the Discloser's corporate finance adviser rather than its staff or customers.

## 6. Return of Information

6.1 On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall within ten days destroy or return every document containing Confidential Information, erase it from its systems so far as reasonably practicable, and have a director confirm in writing that this has been done.

## 7. Duration and Remedies

7.1 The Recipient's obligations under this letter continue for three years from its date, whether or not a sale proceeds.

7.2 The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this letter, and that the Discloser may seek an injunction to prevent or stop one.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 Please sign the copy of this letter where indicated and return it to the Discloser to confirm the Recipient's acceptance of its terms. This letter and any dispute arising from it are governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
