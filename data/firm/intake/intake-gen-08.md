# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Hartwell Agritech Limited and its Affiliates ("Hartwell") and Prairie Rose Seeds Inc. ("Prairie Rose").

1.2 This Agreement is dated 9 March 2026 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Hartwell and Prairie Rose are discussing a possible field trial of drought-tolerant seed varieties (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Hartwell and Prairie Rose will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by inspection, and whether or not marked as confidential, including business plans, financial information, technical data, software, know-how, customer information and trade secrets.

2.2 Confidential Information also includes all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent that they contain or reflect any information described above.

## 3. No Licence

3.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 4. Legally Required Disclosure

4.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 5. Exclusions

5.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 6. Return of Materials

6.1 Upon the expiry or termination of this Agreement, or earlier at the request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and shall certify the destruction in writing if the Disclosing Party asks it to.

## 7. No Representations

7.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

7.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

8.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 9. No Solicitation of Staff

9.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 10. Duration

10.1 This Agreement continues in force until terminated by either party giving the other not less than sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

10.2 The obligations in this Agreement in respect of Confidential Information disclosed before its expiry or termination continue for a further five (5) years after it ends.

10.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario.

11.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Province of Ontario.

11.3 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 12. Remedies

12.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 13. Confidentiality Obligations

13.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 14. Indemnification

14.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

14.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 15. Disclosure to Representatives

15.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

15.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 16. Permitted Purpose

16.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

16.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 17. Miscellaneous

17.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

17.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.
