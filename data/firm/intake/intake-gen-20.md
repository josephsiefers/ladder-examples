# Engagement Letter

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This letter is sent by Whitcombe Lane LLP ("we" or "us") to the board of directors of Fairhaven Foods Limited (the "Company").

1.2 Thank you for appointing us as auditors of the Company. This letter confirms the terms of our engagement to audit the Company's financial statements for the year ending 31 March 2027, explains the responsibilities that the directors and we each have, and sets out our fees. It applies to each later year's audit until it is replaced.

## 2. Scope of the Audit

2.1 Our work will consist of an examination, on a test basis, of evidence for the amounts and disclosures in the financial statements, sufficient for us to form an opinion on whether they are free from material misstatement. We will report that opinion to the members as the Companies Act 2006 requires.

## 3. Responsibilities of the Directors

3.1 Preparing the financial statements is the responsibility of the directors, not us. The directors must also safeguard the Company's assets, keep proper accounting records, and give us unrestricted access to the records, staff and information we ask for. Before we sign our report, the directors will be asked to confirm in writing the representations made to us during the audit.

## 4. Our Responsibilities

4.1 We will plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance that the financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether caused by fraud or error. We will report to the directors any significant weaknesses in internal control that come to our attention.

## 5. Timetable and Staffing

5.1 The audit partner will be Helen Marsh, who is responsible for the conduct of the audit and for signing our report. Fieldwork will take place in June 2027, and we expect to issue our report by the end of July 2027, so long as the Company provides its draft financial statements by the end of May.

## 6. Fees

6.1 Our fee for the audit for the year ending 31 March 2027 is £42,500, excluding VAT and expenses, based on the time we expect to spend. If the Company's records are not ready on time, or the audit takes longer for reasons outside our control, we will discuss an additional fee with the directors before incurring it.

6.2 We will invoice half the fee at the start of fieldwork and the balance when we sign our report, each invoice payable within thirty days.

## 7. Liability and Complaints

7.1 Subject to approval by the Company's members, our liability for any loss caused by our negligence in the audit is limited to £1,000,000 in total. Nothing in this letter limits our liability for fraud or dishonesty.

7.2 If the directors wish to complain about our service, they should write to our managing partner, who will investigate and reply within twenty-eight days.

## 8. Agreement of Terms

8.1 This letter, together with our standard terms of business enclosed with it, forms the contract between the Company and us, and is governed by the law of England and Wales. Please confirm the Company's agreement by having a director sign and return the enclosed copy of this letter.
