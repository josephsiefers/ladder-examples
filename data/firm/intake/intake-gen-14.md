# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Orrin Medical Devices Inc. ("Orrin") and Halcyon Clinical Research LLC ("Halcyon").

1.2 Halcyon (the "Disclosing Party") intends to disclose Confidential Information to Orrin (the "Receiving Party") for a possible clinical study of Halcyon's patient monitoring protocol (the "Purpose"). Orrin will not disclose confidential information under this Agreement, which imposes obligations of confidentiality on Orrin only.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Miscellaneous

3.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

3.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 4. Representatives

4.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 5. Confidentiality Obligations

5.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 This Agreement continues for twelve (12) months and then expires automatically.

6.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement and continue indefinitely, until the Confidential Information falls within one of the exclusions in this Agreement.

6.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 7. No Warranty

7.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 8. Liability

8.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

8.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 9. Information Not Covered

9.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

9.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 10. Equitable Relief

10.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

10.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 11. Legally Required Disclosure

11.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

11.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 12. Return or Destruction

12.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

12.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 13. Non-Solicitation

13.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 14. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

14.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

14.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Republic of Singapore.

14.3 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 15. No Licence

15.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 16. Indemnity

16.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 17. Use of Confidential Information

17.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.
