# Framework Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Master Services Agreement is made between Marchmont Properties Limited (the "Supplier") and Linden Print Ltd (the "Customer").

1.2 The Supplier provides technology consulting, implementation and support services. This agreement sets out the general terms on which the Supplier will provide services to the Customer from time to time. Each engagement will be described in a statement of work signed by both parties, and each statement of work forms a separate contract incorporating these terms.

## 2. Change Control

2.1 The Customer may ask for changes to the services by written change request. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days with the impact of the change, and shall implement it only once the Customer accepts that impact in writing.

## 3. Liability

3.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all statements of work.

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1 Either party may terminate this agreement or any statement of work immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty days of being notified of it.

## 5. Services

5.1 The Supplier shall perform the services described in each statement of work with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and using appropriately qualified personnel.

5.2 The Customer shall give the Supplier reasonable access to its premises, systems and staff as the services require, and shall make decisions promptly where the Supplier needs them.

## 6. Fees and Payment

6.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rates in the relevant statement of work, unless it states a fixed price.

6.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index, on sixty days' written notice.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party may disclose confidential information to the other under this agreement. "Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by a party, including its business plans, pricing, software, data and know-how.

7.2 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only to perform this agreement, and disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

7.3 These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the receiving party, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law. They survive termination of this agreement for five years.
