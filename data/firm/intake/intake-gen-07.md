# Framework Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Gartside Distribution Ltd ("Gartside" or the "Customer") and Pellam Software Limited ("Pellam" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Gartside wishes to appoint Pellam as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Statements of Work

2.1 The Customer may request services at any time by sending the Supplier a draft statement of work. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days, either accepting the draft or proposing changes to it, and no statement of work takes effect until signed by an authorised representative of each party.

2.2 Where a statement of work and these master terms are inconsistent, these master terms take precedence.

## 3. Services

3.1 The Supplier shall provide the services in accordance with each statement of work and any service levels it sets out, and shall comply with the Customer's reasonable site rules when working at the Customer's premises.

3.2 If the Supplier expects to miss a date set out in a statement of work, it shall notify the Customer promptly, explain the cause, and propose a plan to recover the delay.

## 4. Personnel

4.1 The Supplier shall name a delivery manager for each statement of work, who shall be the Customer's day-to-day contact. The Supplier shall not replace a named key person without the Customer's prior consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in each statement of work. Unless a statement of work states otherwise, the Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears and the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt.

5.2 The Customer shall notify any disputed amount within fifteen days of receiving the invoice, giving its reasons, and the parties shall try in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 The Supplier owns its tools, methods and know-how, including any it improves while performing the services. On payment, the Customer owns the deliverables named in each statement of work, and receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence of any Supplier material incorporated in them, for its own business purposes.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit. Each party's aggregate liability under this agreement is capped at one hundred and twenty-five per cent of the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim, save for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Termination of this agreement ends every statement of work then in force unless the parties agree otherwise.

8.2 Either party may terminate this agreement or any statement of work immediately by notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty days of being notified of it.

## 9. General

9.1 Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent. Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses stated in the relevant statement of work. This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
