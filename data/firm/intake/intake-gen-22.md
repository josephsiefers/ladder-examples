1.1 Marchmont Properties Limited ("Marchmont") licenses its LedgerFlow accounting software to Linden Print Ltd ("Linden").

1.2 The licence permits Linden to use the software for up to 150 named users at its own sites. The software is licensed, not sold, and Marchmont keeps ownership of it.

## 2. Licence Fees

2.1 The Licensee shall pay an annual licence fee of GBP 42,000 in advance. Additional users are charged at GBP 300 per user per year.

## 3. Support and Updates

3.1 While the fees are paid, the Licensor shall provide updates and telephone support during business hours, and shall use reasonable efforts to correct reported errors.

## 4. Warranty and Term

4.1 The Licensor warrants that the Software will perform substantially in accordance with its documentation for ninety days from installation. The licence continues until terminated for breach or on twelve months' notice.

## 5. Licence Restrictions

5.1 The Licensee shall not copy, modify, decompile or sublicense the Software, except as the law expressly allows, and shall not use it to provide services to third parties.
