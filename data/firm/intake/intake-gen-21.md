# Data Processing Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This addendum is entered into by Tamsin Software Inc. ("Tamsin") and its customer Redwater Foods Limited ("Redwater").

1.2 Tamsin processes personal data as a processor for Redwater, which is the controller of that data. This addendum governs that processing: the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1.

## 2. Sub-processors

2.1 The Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in Annex 3. The Processor shall impose on each sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this agreement, and remains liable for their performance.

## 3. Data Subject Rights and Deletion

3.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights.

3.2 At the end of the services the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data, unless the law requires it to be kept.

## 4. Personal Data Breaches

4.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach, and shall give the Controller the information it needs to meet its own reporting obligations.

## 5. Security and Personnel

5.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the personal data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and regular testing.

5.2 The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

## 6. Processing on Instructions

6.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required to do so by law, in which case it shall inform the Controller of that requirement first.
