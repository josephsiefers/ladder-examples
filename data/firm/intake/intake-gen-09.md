# Mutual Confidentiality Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Confidentiality Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Sunstone Learning Pte. Ltd. ("Sunstone") and Arden Publishing Limited ("Arden"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on September 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Sunstone and Arden are discussing a possible co-publishing arrangement for school mathematics courses (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Sunstone and Arden will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically or by inspection, and whether or not marked as confidential, including business plans, financial information, technical data, software, know-how, customer information and trade secrets.

2.2 Confidential Information also includes all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent that they contain or reflect any information described above.

## 3. Limitation of Liability

3.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 4. Standard of Care

4.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

4.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 5. Equitable Relief

5.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

5.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 6. Return or Destruction

6.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

6.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Use of Confidential Information

7.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 8. Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

8.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

8.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 9. No Licence

9.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 10. Representatives

10.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 11. Non-Solicitation

11.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 12. Compelled Disclosure

12.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 13. No Representations

13.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

13.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 14. Indemnification

14.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

14.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 15. Miscellaneous

15.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

15.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 16. Term and Termination

16.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of one (1) year from the date on which it takes effect, unless terminated earlier by agreement in writing.

16.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality shall remain in force for a period of two (2) years from the date on which this Agreement expires or terminates.

16.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 17. Information Not Covered

17.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

17.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.
