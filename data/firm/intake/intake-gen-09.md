# Letter of Engagement

Dear Calder & Finch,

1. Thank you for instructing Northcote Engineering Limited to act for Calder & Finch LLP in connection with its proposed refinancing.

2. This letter confirms the terms of our engagement: the scope of the work we will do for you, who will do it, and how our fees will be charged.

3. Our fees are charged at hourly rates between GBP 180 and GBP 520 and are billed monthly.

4. Our liability to you for this engagement is limited to GBP 5 million. Please sign and return a copy of this letter to confirm your agreement.

5. We will carry out the work in accordance with applicable auditing standards and report to the members of the company.

Yours sincerely, Northcote Engineering Limited
