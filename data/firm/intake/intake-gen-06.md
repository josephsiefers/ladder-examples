# Statement of Work No. 3

## 1. Background

1.1 This Statement of Work is issued under the master services agreement between Ostler Consulting LLP (the "Supplier") and Wrenfield Foods Limited (the "Customer"), and incorporates its terms.

1.2 This Statement of Work describes one project: the Supplier will implement its warehouse management system at the Customer's distribution centre. It sets out the scope, the deliverables, the timetable, the fees and the responsibilities of each party for that project only.

## 2. Scope of Work

2.1 The Supplier shall configure its warehouse management system for the Customer's distribution centre, build an interface between it and the Customer's order management system, migrate the Customer's current stock records into it, and train the Customer's staff to use it.

2.2 Hardware, network changes at the distribution centre, and changes to the Customer's order management system are outside the scope of this Statement of Work.

## 3. Deliverables

3.1 The Supplier shall deliver: a design specification describing the configuration and the interface; the configured system, tested and ready for go-live; and a training and handover pack, with two days of on-site training for up to twelve of the Customer's staff.

## 4. Timetable and Milestones

4.1 Work starts on the first working day after this Statement of Work is signed. The Supplier shall deliver the design specification by the end of week four (Milestone 1), the configured system for testing by the end of week twelve (Milestone 2), and go-live with the training and handover pack by the end of week sixteen (Milestone 3).

## 5. Acceptance

5.1 The Customer shall review each deliverable within ten working days of receiving it, and either accept it in writing or list the ways in which it fails to meet this Statement of Work. The Supplier shall correct any listed failure and resubmit the deliverable for review. A milestone is accepted when the Customer accepts every deliverable due at it.

## 6. Fees and Payment

6.1 The fixed price for this project is £186,000, excluding VAT, paid in three instalments: £37,200 on acceptance of Milestone 1, £93,000 on acceptance of Milestone 2, and the final instalment of £55,800 on acceptance of Milestone 3.

6.2 The Supplier shall invoice each instalment when its milestone is accepted, and the Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty days of receipt.

## 7. Customer Responsibilities

7.1 The Customer shall give the Supplier's consultants access to the distribution centre during working hours, provide test data and a test environment for its order management system, and make its warehouse supervisors available for workshops and training. The Supplier is not responsible for any delay caused by the Customer's failure to do so.

## 8. Project Governance

8.1 Each party shall appoint a project manager. The project managers shall meet weekly to review progress against the milestones, record risks and agree actions, and the Supplier shall circulate a short written report after each meeting.

## 9. Changes and Term

9.1 Any change to the scope, deliverables, timetable or fees of this Statement of Work must be agreed in a written change note signed by both parties. This Statement of Work ends when Milestone 3 is accepted, unless it is terminated earlier under the master services agreement.
