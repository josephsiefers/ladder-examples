# Data Processing Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement is made between Redwater Foods Limited (the "Controller") and Tamsin Software Inc. (the "Processor").

1.2 The Processor provides services to the Controller under a separate services agreement, and in doing so processes personal data on the Controller's behalf. This agreement sets out the terms on which the Processor processes that personal data, as Article 28 of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR require.

## 2. Data Subject Rights and Deletion

2.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights.

2.2 At the end of the services the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data, unless the law requires it to be kept.

## 3. Personal Data Breaches

3.1 On becoming aware of a personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller within twenty-four hours, take reasonable steps to contain it, and cooperate with the Controller's investigation.

## 4. Processing on Instructions

4.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required to do so by law, in which case it shall inform the Controller of that requirement first.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in Annex 3. The Processor shall impose on each sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this agreement, and remains liable for their performance.

## 6. Security and Personnel

6.1 The Processor shall maintain the security measures described in Annex 2 and shall not materially reduce their overall level of protection during the term.
