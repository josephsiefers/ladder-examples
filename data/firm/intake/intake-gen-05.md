# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Corvid Therapeutics, Inc. ("Corvid") and Lantern Ridge Capital LLC ("Lantern Ridge"), each a "party".

1.2 This Agreement is made on the 14th day of July 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Corvid and Lantern Ridge have engaged in mutual discussions concerning a possible minority investment by Lantern Ridge in Corvid (the "Purpose").

1.4 To allow those discussions to continue, Corvid (the "Disclosing Party") is prepared to make Confidential Information available to Lantern Ridge (the "Receiving Party"). Lantern Ridge will not provide confidential information under this Agreement, and only Lantern Ridge is bound by obligations of confidentiality.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Standard of Care

3.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

3.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 4. Use of Confidential Information

4.1 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, and shall not use it to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.

## 5. Information Not Covered

5.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

5.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 6. Indemnity

6.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 7. Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the State of New York.

7.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

7.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 8. Compelled Disclosure

8.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 9. No Warranty

9.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 10. Remedies

10.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

11.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 12. Liability

12.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

12.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 13. Term and Termination

13.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of two (2) years from the date on which it takes effect, unless terminated earlier by agreement in writing.

13.2 The obligations in this Agreement in respect of Confidential Information disclosed before its expiry or termination continue for a further eighteen (18) months after it ends.

13.3 The expiry or termination of this Agreement does not affect any rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties that have accrued before it takes effect.

## 14. No Solicitation of Staff

14.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 15. Representatives

15.1 "Representatives" means the officers, employees, legal advisers, accountants and financial advisers of the Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall limit disclosure of the Confidential Information to those Representatives who require it for the Purpose, and shall ensure that they comply with this Agreement as if they were party to it.

## 16. Return of Materials

16.1 Upon the expiry or termination of this Agreement, or earlier at the request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control and shall certify the destruction in writing if the Disclosing Party asks it to.

## 17. No Licence

17.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.
