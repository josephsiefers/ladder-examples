# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Veridian Payments Pte. Ltd. ("Veridian") and Saltmarsh Analytics Limited and its Affiliates ("Saltmarsh").

1.2 This Agreement takes effect on November 30, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Veridian (the "Disclosing Party") intends to disclose Confidential Information to Saltmarsh (the "Receiving Party") for a possible fraud-scoring pilot using Veridian's transaction data (the "Purpose"). Saltmarsh will not disclose confidential information under this Agreement, which imposes obligations of confidentiality on Saltmarsh only.

## 2. Evaluation Material

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all information concerning the business, operations, products, technology or finances of the Disclosing Party that is furnished to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, whether before or after the signing of this Agreement, together with any trade secrets of the Disclosing Party.

2.2 Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party at all times.

## 3. Miscellaneous

3.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

3.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 4. Remedies

4.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 5. Standard of Care

5.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

5.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 6. No Licence

6.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 7. Compelled Disclosure

7.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue or goodwill, save that nothing in this Agreement limits the liability of the Receiving Party for a breach of its obligations of confidentiality.

## 9. Return or Destruction

9.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

9.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 10. No Representations

10.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

10.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 11. No Solicitation of Staff

11.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 12. Duration

12.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on sixty (60) days' written notice to the other; until then it remains in force.

12.2 The obligations in this Agreement in respect of Confidential Information disclosed before its expiry or termination continue for a further two (2) years after it ends.

12.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 13. Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

13.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

13.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 14. Permitted Purpose

14.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

14.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 15. Information Not Covered

15.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

15.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 16. Indemnification

16.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

16.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 17. Disclosure to Representatives

17.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

17.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.
