# Settlement Agreement: Without Prejudice

Dear Sirs,

1. We write on behalf of Harrow Analytics Ltd to record the terms on which it and Belgrave Retail Group plc have agreed to settle the dispute over unpaid invoices for the 2026 logistics services.

2. Once you countersign this letter, it is a binding agreement in full and final settlement of all claims between our clients arising from those invoices.

3. The claimant shall discontinue the claim within five working days of receiving the final instalment.

4. On receipt of the settlement sum, each party releases the other from all claims arising from the matters in dispute, whether known or unknown.

5. Belgrave shall pay Harrow GBP 212,500 within fourteen days of the date of this letter, in full and final settlement.

Yours faithfully, Harrow Analytics Ltd
