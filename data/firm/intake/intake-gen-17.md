# Confidentiality Letter

## 1. Purpose of this Letter

1.1 This letter is sent by Calloway Freight Holdings plc (the "Discloser") to Dunmore Capital Partners LLP (the "Recipient") and, once countersigned by the Recipient, is a binding agreement between them.

1.2 The Discloser is considering selling its logistics software division, and the Recipient wishes to evaluate a possible purchase of it (the "Purpose"). The Discloser is willing to share confidential information about the division with the Recipient for the Purpose only, and only on the condition that the Recipient keeps it confidential as this letter requires.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 In this letter, "Confidential Information" means all information about the division, its customers, contracts, software, finances and staff that the Discloser or its advisers give the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, together with the fact that a sale is being considered.

2.2 It does not include information that is or becomes public other than through the Recipient's breach, that the Recipient already lawfully held, or that it receives from a third party free to disclose it.

## 3. Undertakings

3.1 The Recipient undertakes not to use the Confidential Information except for the Purpose, not to disclose it to any person except as permitted below, and to keep it secure against unauthorised access, at least to the standard it applies to its own commercially sensitive information.

## 4. Permitted Disclosure

4.1 Disclosure is permitted only to those of the Recipient's officers, staff, financiers and advisers who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by duties of confidentiality, and to the extent required by law, court order or the rules of a stock exchange. The Recipient is liable for any breach by those to whom it discloses.

## 5. No Approach to Staff or Customers

5.1 While the Purpose is pursued, and for twelve months after, the Recipient shall not solicit any of the division's employees whom it met or learned of through the Confidential Information, and shall direct all enquiries about the division to the Discloser's corporate finance adviser rather than its staff or customers.

## 6. Return of Information

6.1 If the Discloser asks, or the Recipient decides not to proceed, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and any copies, and confirm in writing that it has done so, except copies it must keep by law, which remain subject to this letter.

## 7. Duration and Remedies

7.1 The Recipient's obligations under this letter continue for three years from its date, whether or not a sale proceeds.

7.2 The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this letter, and that the Discloser may seek an injunction to prevent or stop one.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 Please sign the copy of this letter where indicated and return it to the Discloser to confirm the Recipient's acceptance of its terms. This letter and any dispute arising from it are governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
