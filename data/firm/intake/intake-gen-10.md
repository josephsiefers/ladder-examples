# Statement of Work

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 Veridian Payments Ltd ("Veridian") and Ashby Wholesale Limited ("Ashby") agree this Statement of Work under their existing master services agreement, which governs it.

1.2 Under this Statement of Work Ashby will design and deliver a customer self-service portal for Veridian. Nothing in it changes the master agreement except where it expressly says so.

## 2. Milestones

2.1 The project starts on the date both parties sign this Statement of Work. Design sign-off is due in week three, user acceptance testing begins in week nine and go-live is planned for week twelve.

## 3. Project Governance

3.1 A steering group of one senior representative of each party shall meet monthly and resolve any issue the project managers cannot.

## 4. Fees

4.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rate card in the master agreement, capped at USD 240,000. The Supplier shall report hours weekly and seek approval before exceeding eighty per cent of the cap.

## 5. Scope and Deliverables

5.1 The Supplier shall deliver: (a) a migration plan; (b) the migrated system in a test environment; (c) the migrated system in production; and (d) handover documentation and two training sessions for the Customer's staff.

5.2 Out of scope: changes to the Customer's finance systems and any data cleansing beyond de-duplication.

## 6. Acceptance

6.1 The Customer shall test each deliverable within ten working days of delivery and either accept it or list the ways in which it fails the acceptance criteria. A deliverable not rejected within that period is treated as accepted.
