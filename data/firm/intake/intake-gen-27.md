# Master Services and Consulting Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Orrin Biotech Limited ("Orrin" or the "Customer") and Elmstead Packaging Limited ("Elmstead" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Orrin wishes to appoint Elmstead as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Termination of this agreement ends every statement of work then in force unless the parties agree otherwise.

## 3. Change Control

3.1 Either party may request a change to a statement of work. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change note stating its effect on scope, timetable and fees.

## 4. Liability

4.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all statements of work.

## 5. Fees and Payment

5.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rates in the relevant statement of work, unless it states a fixed price.

5.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index, on sixty days' written notice.

## 6. Services

6.1 The Supplier shall perform the services described in each statement of work with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice, and using appropriately qualified personnel.

6.2 The Customer shall give the Supplier reasonable access to its premises, systems and staff as the services require, and shall make decisions promptly where the Supplier needs them.
