# Lease

## 1. Parties and Premises

1.1 Kingsmere Estates Limited ("Kingsmere" or the "Landlord") grants this lease to Fallow & Pike LLP ("Fallow & Pike" or the "Tenant").

1.2 By this lease Kingsmere lets the office premises on the second floor of Harcourt House, 14 Quayside Road, Bristol (the "Premises") to Fallow & Pike for five years from 1 March 2027 (the "Term"), for use as offices. The Tenant may use the common parts of the building, including the entrance hall, lifts, stairs and toilets, in common with the Landlord's other tenants.

## 2. Rent

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a yearly rent of £84,000, excluding VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days, the first payment to be made on 1 March 2027 for the period up to the next quarter day.

2.2 If any rent is unpaid fourteen days after it falls due, the Tenant shall pay interest on it at four per cent above the Bank of England base rate until it is paid.

## 3. Service Charge and Outgoings

3.1 In addition to the rent, the Tenant shall pay, as a service charge, a fair proportion of the Landlord's costs of repairing, cleaning, lighting and insuring the building and its common parts. The Landlord shall send the Tenant an estimate before each year and a certified statement of actual costs after it.

3.2 The Tenant shall also pay all business rates and utility charges for the Premises.

## 4. Use

4.1 The Premises may be used only as offices within Class E of the Use Classes Order. The Tenant shall comply with the regulations the Landlord reasonably makes for all occupiers of the building, and shall not hold any auction, sale or public meeting at the Premises.

## 5. Repair and Alterations

5.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair and decorative condition, but need not put them in a better state than that shown in the schedule of condition the parties signed with this lease.

5.2 The Tenant shall make no structural alterations. It may install or move internal partitions with the Landlord's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. Assignment and Underletting

6.1 The Tenant shall not assign or underlet the whole of the Premises without the Landlord's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, and shall not assign or underlet part only of them. The Tenant may share occupation with a company in its group, provided no tenancy is created.

## 7. Break Option

7.1 The Tenant may end this lease on the third anniversary of the start of the Term by giving the Landlord at least six months' written notice, provided it has paid all rent due and gives up the Premises free of any occupiers on the break date.

## 8. Landlord's Obligations and Forfeiture

8.1 So long as the Tenant pays the rent and complies with this lease, it may occupy the Premises without interruption by the Landlord, who shall keep the building insured. The Landlord may end this lease by re-entering the Premises if any rent is twenty-one days overdue, if the Tenant breaks a covenant in this lease and does not remedy it, or if the Tenant becomes insolvent.

## 9. End of the Lease

9.1 When this lease ends, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises, return all keys and access cards, and remove any alterations the Landlord asks it to remove, making good the Premises afterwards. Before this lease was granted, the parties excluded the Tenant's right to a new lease under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954.
