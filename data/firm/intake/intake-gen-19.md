# Master Services Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This agreement is entered into by Ostrava Health Ltd ("Ostrava" or the "Customer") and Quillon Cloud Services Limited ("Quillon" or the "Supplier").

1.2 Ostrava wishes to appoint Quillon as a non-exclusive provider of professional services over the term of this agreement. No services are ordered by this agreement itself: the Customer orders specific work by signing a statement of work, which states its scope, deliverables, timetable and fees, and these master terms govern every statement of work.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the services in accordance with each statement of work and any service levels it sets out. If the Supplier expects to miss a milestone it shall notify the Customer promptly and propose a recovery plan.

## 3. Liability

3.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, each party's total liability in each contract year is limited to the fees paid or payable in that year under all statements of work.

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1 This agreement runs for three years and then continues until either party gives six months' written notice. Termination of this agreement ends every statement of work then in force unless the parties agree otherwise.

## 5. Change Control

5.1 The Customer may ask for changes to the services by written change request. The Supplier shall respond within ten working days with the impact of the change, and shall implement it only once the Customer accepts that impact in writing.

## 6. Fees and Payment

6.1 Fees are charged on a time and materials basis at the rates in the relevant statement of work, unless it states a fixed price.

6.2 The Supplier may increase its rates once in each contract year by no more than the increase in the Consumer Prices Index, on sixty days' written notice.

## 7. Schedule 1: Data Processing Terms

7.1 To the extent the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer in performing the services, the Customer is the controller and the Supplier is the processor, and this Schedule applies.

7.2 The Supplier shall process that personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, ensure its personnel are bound by confidentiality, and implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

7.3 The Supplier shall not engage a sub-processor without the Customer's prior written authorisation, and shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach without undue delay and within forty-eight hours.

7.4 At the end of the services the Supplier shall delete or return all personal data, and shall make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the UK GDPR.
