# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and between Brannock Freight Inc. ("Brannock") and Elmstead Software LLC ("Elmstead"), each a "party".

1.2 Elmstead (the "Disclosing Party") intends to disclose Confidential Information to Brannock (the "Receiving Party") for a possible pilot of Elmstead's route-planning software (the "Purpose"). Brannock will not disclose confidential information under this Agreement, which imposes obligations of confidentiality on Brannock only.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Non-Solicitation

3.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it has had contact in connection with the Purpose, provided that general advertisements not directed at such employees shall not breach this clause.

## 4. Miscellaneous

4.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

4.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

## 5. Equitable Relief

5.1 Any unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm. The Disclosing Party may therefore apply for an injunction to restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement without proving actual damage.

5.2 The rights and remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any rights or remedies provided by law.

## 6. Permitted Purpose

6.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

6.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 7. Disclosure to Representatives

7.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

7.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 8. Legally Required Disclosure

8.1 Where the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, where permitted, notify the Disclosing Party promptly so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

8.2 The Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party reasonable assistance, at the Disclosing Party's cost, in seeking any such order.

## 9. Standard of Care

9.1 The Receiving Party shall hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall store it securely, and shall take all reasonable steps to prevent its unauthorised disclosure, copying or use.

9.2 The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

10.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 11. No Licence

11.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 12. Indemnification

12.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Disclosing Party from and against any claims, losses and reasonable costs arising out of an unauthorised disclosure or use of Confidential Information by the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives.

12.2 The Disclosing Party shall notify the Receiving Party promptly of any claim for which it seeks indemnity and shall take reasonable steps to mitigate its loss.

## 13. No Representations

13.1 The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Receiving Party or its Representatives resulting from their use of it.

13.2 Only those representations and warranties that are made in a definitive written agreement, when and if signed, shall have any legal effect.

## 14. Duration

14.1 This Agreement continues in force until terminated by either party giving the other not less than ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

14.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for three (3) years.

14.3 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 15. Exclusions

15.1 The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in its possession free of any duty of confidence before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party; (c) is lawfully received from a third party who is free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed by or for the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 16. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

16.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware.

16.2 Nothing in this clause prevents the Disclosing Party from applying for injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 17. Return or Destruction

17.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

17.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 18. Non-Solicitation of Customers and Non-Competition

18.1 Neither party shall, during the term of this Agreement and for eighteen months after it ends, approach, canvass or deal with any customer or supplier of the other party that it was introduced to, or learnt about, in the course of the discussions under this Agreement, with a view to taking business away from the other party.

18.2 During the same period, neither party shall set up, invest in or work for any business that competes with the other party in the market for the products discussed, except by holding less than three per cent of the shares of a listed company.

18.3 The parties consider each of these restrictions to be separate and reasonable. If a court finds one of them too wide, the others continue to apply.
