# Contract of Employment

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Contract of Employment is between Elmstead Packaging Limited (the "Company") and Priya Raman (the "Employee").

1.2 The Company employs the Employee as Head of Product from the start date set out below. This contract sets out the terms and conditions of that employment and is the written statement of particulars that employment law requires.

## 2. Pay and Benefits

2.1 The Employee's salary is GBP 98,000 a year, paid monthly in arrears. The Employee is eligible for the Company's annual bonus scheme and pension scheme, to which the Company contributes six per cent of salary.

## 3. Duties and Place of Work

3.1 The Employee shall perform the duties the Company reasonably assigns, and shall report to the Chief Executive. The normal place of work is the Company's London office, with up to two days a week of home working.

## 4. Hours and Holiday

4.1 The Employee works thirty-seven and a half hours a week and is entitled to twenty-five days' holiday plus public holidays. Up to five unused days may be carried forward.

## 5. Notice and Termination

5.1 After probation, either party may end the employment by giving three months' written notice. The Company may pay salary in lieu of notice.
