# Project Statement of Work

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Statement of Work is made under the Master Services Agreement between Ashby Wholesale Limited (the "Supplier") and Veridian Payments Ltd (the "Customer"), and incorporates its terms.

1.2 This Statement of Work orders one project: the migration of the Customer's warehouse inventory system to the Supplier's cloud platform. It sets out that project's scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria and fees.

## 2. Project Governance

2.1 Each party appoints a project manager. They shall meet weekly to review progress, risks and change requests.

## 3. Scope and Deliverables

3.1 Deliverables: a design document, a working portal with user login, order tracking and returns, integration with the Customer's order database, and a runbook for the Customer's support team.

## 4. Fees

4.1 The fixed price for this Statement of Work is GBP 186,000, invoiced as follows: 20% on milestone 1, 30% on milestone 2 and 50% on milestone 3.

## 5. Acceptance

5.1 Acceptance testing follows the test plan agreed at design sign-off. The Supplier shall fix any severity 1 or 2 defect found in testing before go-live.

## 6. Milestones

6.1 The project starts on the date both parties sign this Statement of Work. Design sign-off is due in week three, user acceptance testing begins in week nine and go-live is planned for week twelve.
