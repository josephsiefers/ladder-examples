# Settlement Letter

## 1. Background

1.1 This letter is written by Sterling Rack Installations Limited (the "Supplier") to Greyfold Distribution Limited (the "Customer") and, once countersigned by the Customer, records a binding agreement between them.

1.2 The Supplier installed pallet racking at the Customer's warehouse in Northampton in 2026 and invoiced £312,000 for it. The Customer withheld £118,000, alleging that parts of the racking were defective and were installed late, and the Supplier has issued a claim for that sum in the County Court (together, the "Dispute"). The parties wish to settle the Dispute in full, without any admission of liability, on the terms of this letter.

## 2. Settlement Payment

2.1 In full and final settlement of the Dispute, the Customer shall pay the Supplier the sum of £85,000 (the "Settlement Sum"), inclusive of all interest and legal costs, within fourteen days of countersigning this letter. Payment shall be made in cleared funds to the Supplier's usual bank account.

## 3. Remedial Works

3.1 Within thirty days after receiving the Settlement Sum, the Supplier shall replace the twelve damaged upright frames at the warehouse, at its own cost, at times agreed with the Customer's site manager so that the Customer's operations are not disrupted. When the work is done, the Supplier shall give the Customer a load inspection certificate for the racking.

## 4. Release of Claims

4.1 This settlement is in full and final satisfaction of every claim either party has, or may have, against the other in connection with the Dispute, the racking or its installation. Neither party shall bring any further claim on those matters, save for a claim for breach of the terms in this letter.

## 5. Court Proceedings

5.1 Within seven days of receiving the Settlement Sum, the Supplier shall file with the court a notice of discontinuance of its claim, and each party shall bear its own costs of the proceedings. Neither party shall take any further step in the proceedings, except to enforce this letter.

## 6. No Admission

6.1 This settlement is a compromise. Neither party admits liability for any part of the Dispute: the Customer's payment of the Settlement Sum is not an admission that the racking was free of defects, nor are the Supplier's remedial works an admission that it was defective.

## 7. General

7.1 The parties confirm that they have each taken legal advice on this letter. It records their entire agreement on the Dispute, it is governed by English law, and it may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original.

## 8. Acceptance

8.1 If the Customer agrees to these terms, please have an authorised signatory sign and date the enclosed copy of this letter and return it to the Supplier. The agreement becomes binding when the Supplier receives the countersigned copy.
