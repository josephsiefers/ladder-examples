# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Tidewell Energy Systems Limited and its Affiliates ("Tidewell") and Quillon Software Ltd ("Quillon").

1.2 This Agreement comes into force on the date of the last signature below (the "Effective Date").

1.3 Tidewell and Quillon are discussing a possible integration of Quillon's scheduling software with Tidewell's battery controllers (the "Purpose").

1.4 Each of Tidewell and Quillon will disclose Confidential Information to the other and will protect the Confidential Information it receives. References to the "Disclosing Party" and the "Receiving Party" are to each of them in that capacity.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

2.1 In this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information of a confidential nature, in whatever form, that the Disclosing Party or its Representatives make available to the Receiving Party for the Purpose, including information that is marked as confidential and information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

## 3. Law and Jurisdiction

3.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the State of California.

3.2 Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of California.

3.3 This clause applies equally to any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether contractual or non-contractual.

## 4. Liability

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

4.2 Subject to the preceding paragraph, neither party shall be liable for any special, indirect or consequential loss arising out of this Agreement, other than loss arising from an unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

5.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose any Confidential Information, it may do so provided that, to the extent lawful, it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice of the requirement, consults with the Disclosing Party on the form of the disclosure, and discloses only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose.

## 6. Duration

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice to the other; until then it remains in force.

6.2 The Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for three (3) years.

6.3 In respect of any Confidential Information that is a trade secret, those obligations continue indefinitely, for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

6.4 Termination or expiry of this Agreement shall not release the Receiving Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement committed before it takes effect.

## 7. Return or Destruction

7.1 On the written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and, if requested, confirm in writing that it has done so.

7.2 The Receiving Party may keep copies that it is required to keep by law or regulation, or that are held in automatic electronic back-up systems, provided that they remain subject to this Agreement.

## 8. Permitted Purpose

8.1 The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for the Purpose.

8.2 The Receiving Party shall not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any sample, prototype or software included in the Confidential Information, except as the Purpose strictly requires.

## 9. Confidentiality Obligations

9.1 The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential and shall protect it using at least the degree of care that it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable degree of care.

## 10. No Solicitation of Staff

10.1 The Receiving Party shall not, while this Agreement remains in force, induce or attempt to induce any officer or senior employee of the Disclosing Party who is involved in the Purpose to leave the employment of the Disclosing Party.

## 11. Information Not Covered

11.1 Confidential Information does not include information which is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives, which was available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure, or which the Receiving Party develops independently without reference to the Confidential Information.

11.2 The Receiving Party bears the burden of showing that any of these exclusions applies.

## 12. Indemnity

12.1 The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against all losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees, that the Disclosing Party incurs as a direct result of any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives.

## 13. Remedies

13.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this Agreement, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance or other equitable relief for any threatened or actual breach, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 14. No Warranty

14.1 All Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Disclosing Party gives no warranty or representation, express or implied, as to its accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose.

## 15. No Licence

15.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or other right under any patent, copyright, trade mark, trade secret or other intellectual property right is granted to the Receiving Party by this Agreement or by any disclosure of Confidential Information.

## 16. Disclosure to Representatives

16.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and contractors (its "Representatives") who need to know it for the Purpose, provided that each of them is informed of its confidential nature and is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Agreement.

16.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would be a breach of this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

## 17. Miscellaneous

17.1 No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of it. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

17.2 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.
