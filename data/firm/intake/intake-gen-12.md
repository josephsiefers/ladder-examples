# Data Sharing Agreement

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Data Processing Agreement is made between Kittering Freight Ltd (the "Controller") and Saltmarsh Media Limited (the "Processor").

1.2 The Processor provides services to the Controller under a separate services agreement, and in doing so processes personal data on the Controller's behalf. This agreement sets out the terms on which the Processor processes that personal data, as Article 28 of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR require.

## 2. Data Subject Rights and Deletion

2.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights.

2.2 At the end of the services the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data, unless the law requires it to be kept.

## 3. Processing on Instructions

3.1 The Processor shall not process the personal data for any purpose of its own. It shall immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes data protection law.

## 4. Security and Personnel

4.1 The Processor shall maintain the security measures described in Annex 2 and shall not materially reduce their overall level of protection during the term.

## 5. Personal Data Breaches

5.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach, and shall give the Controller the information it needs to meet its own reporting obligations.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Processor shall not engage another processor without the Controller's prior general written authorisation. The Processor shall give thirty days' notice of any new sub-processor, and the Controller may object on reasonable grounds.
