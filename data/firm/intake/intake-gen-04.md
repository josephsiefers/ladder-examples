# Statement of Work

## 1. Parties and Background

1.1 This Statement of Work is made under the Master Services Agreement between Quillon Cloud Services Limited (the "Supplier") and Ostrava Health Ltd (the "Customer"), and incorporates its terms.

1.2 This Statement of Work orders one project: the migration of the Customer's warehouse inventory system to the Supplier's cloud platform. It sets out that project's scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria and fees.

## 2. Acceptance

2.1 The Customer shall test each deliverable within ten working days of delivery and either accept it or list the ways in which it fails the acceptance criteria. A deliverable not rejected within that period is treated as accepted.

## 3. Milestones

3.1 Milestone 1, migration plan approved: four weeks from the start date. Milestone 2, test environment ready: ten weeks. Milestone 3, production go-live: sixteen weeks.

## 4. Project Confidentiality

4.1 The Supplier shall treat as confidential all Customer data, inventory records and plans it receives for this project, use them only for this project, and disclose them only to project staff who need to know them.

4.2 On completion of the project the Supplier shall return or securely destroy all such information. This obligation survives for three years after completion.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 The Supplier retains all intellectual property in its pre-existing tools, software and methods. Intellectual property in deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this Statement of Work vests in the Customer on payment.

5.2 The Supplier grants the Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use any Supplier materials incorporated in the deliverables, for its internal business purposes.

## 6. Scope and Deliverables

6.1 Deliverables: a design document, a working portal with user login, order tracking and returns, integration with the Customer's order database, and a runbook for the Customer's support team.

## 7. Project Governance

7.1 A steering group of one senior representative of each party shall meet monthly and resolve any issue the project managers cannot.

## 8. Fees

8.1 The fixed price for this Statement of Work is GBP 186,000, invoiced as follows: 20% on milestone 1, 30% on milestone 2 and 50% on milestone 3.
